# 📋 Lotus International — Lead Qualification Matrix Questionnaire

> **Purpose:** This questionnaire defines the automated rules that our lead handling system (API, Zoho CRM, and SalesIQ) will use to qualify website enquiries into 3 priority tiers:
> - 🟢 **HIGH PRIORITY (Green):** Instant routing to senior sales rep + priority WhatsApp follow-up.
> - 🟡 **MANUAL REVIEW (Yellow):** Sent to merchandising desk queue for standard review within 1 business day.
> - 🔴 **LOW PRIORITY / AUTO-FILTER (Red):** Standard polite automated response; de-prioritized in sales pipeline.
